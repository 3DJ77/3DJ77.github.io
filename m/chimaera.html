CHIMAERA

The hands and the helm. Every window steers by one shared feel.

UNDERSEA CURRENT — what moves beneath the modules, not a tool you open

Chimaera is the camera and input layer every 3D window in the suite is built on. Orbit, pan, zoom, selection and the on-screen puck behave identically in Structor, Labyrinth and the rest, because they are the same code.

The purpose: learn the feel once. A new module should never make your hands relearn the viewport.

Not downloadable on its own. It ships inside the modules that use it.
