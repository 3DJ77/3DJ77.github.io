TIAMAT

The host. Every tool on one board — putting chaos to work.

UNDERSEA CURRENT — what moves beneath the modules, not a tool you open

Tiamat is the board the whole suite runs on. It knows which machines are on the shop network, which one has the graphics card, which are asleep, and what is running where. Modules ask Tiamat for a place to run; none of them talk to the hardware directly.

The purpose is simple: you can add a second box, or lose one, and the modules keep working without being told anything.

Not downloadable on its own. It ships inside the modules that use it.
