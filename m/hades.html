HADES

The keeper. Giants sleep in the vault; one wakes when called.

UNDERSEA CURRENT — what moves beneath the modules, not a tool you open

Hades keeps the large models. They sleep on disk; a module names the one it needs, Hades wakes it, keeps it resident while it is in use, and parks it again when the card is wanted for something else. Modules never load models themselves.

The purpose: a machine with one graphics card can serve image generation, mesh work and an advisor in turn, without anyone managing memory by hand.

Not downloadable on its own. It ships inside the modules that use it.
