HADES

Hades rules the house of sleepers: every soul is his to keep, released one at a time, and he always gets them back. A 128 GB unified-memory host holds giants the same way — a 123B coder, a 120B advisor, an image model — but only some may be awake at once. Somebody has to keep the ledger of who sleeps and who walks.

The keeper. Giants sleep in the vault; one wakes when called.

IN ACTION

What it is

HADES is the resident-model keeper on the GPU host. sd-serve <key> wakes a model and holds it resident behind a local API; waking one giant may mean sleeping another, and Hades does the bookkeeping — always under the Styx lease, so a wake never tramples a running job. Morpheus and GilgaMESH call through it for FLUX; the advisor lanes hold their LLMs the same way. The session below is real: FLUX woken for a render, then parked, and the resident coder restored — the keeper always gets them back.
