LABYRINTH

STILL IN THE DEEP — surfacing when it clears the bench

The Labyrinth was Daedalus’s masterwork, and it outlived its maker. A toolpath is a labyrinth: one continuous path threading a bounded space, and getting it wrong costs you. This viewer walks the maze first.

The viewer. Walk the maze before the machine does.

Labyrinth reads what the rest of the suite produces — the G-code a post wrote, the sliced file a resin job made — and draws it in 3D so you can eyeball the path before metal or resin is on the line. Moves are drawn as solid tubes, not hairlines, so the path reads from any camera angle.

It is a viewer only. It never edits, never sends, never talks to a controller. Its job is to let you see the mistake on the screen instead of on the part.
